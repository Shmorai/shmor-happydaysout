Theme parks

Disneyland Paris

Marne-la-Vallée, near Paris, France

Disneyland Park plus Disney Adventure World (the renamed Walt Disney Studios Park), where World of Frozen opened on 29 March 2026.

All agesWorld of FrozenUnder-3s free
  • Children under 3 go free
  • Check the official offers page: Disneyland Paris often runs seasonal hotel and ticket offers
  • UK tour operators sometimes include free child places on packages

Scout says: Go for at least two days so you can do both parks without rushing. Frozen fans should head to World of Frozen early.

Fairy-tale theme park

Efteling

Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands

One of Europe’s most-loved parks, famous for its enchanted Fairytale Forest, plus coasters for older kids and grown-ups.

All agesFairy talesEasy from the UK
  • Date-based online tickets: quieter days cost less
  • Holiday parks nearby can work out cheaper than on-site hotels for longer stays

Scout says: The Fairytale Forest is a walk, not a ride, so it works brilliantly for toddlers and grandparents alike.

Theme park resort

Europa-Park

Rust, Germany

Germany’s largest theme park, themed by European countries, with the Rulantica indoor water park next door.

All agesHuge parkWater park next door
  • Multi-day stays make more sense than a single day here
  • Combine with Rulantica on a separate day rather than squeezing both into one

Scout says: It’s big! Two days lets you enjoy it without sprinting between countries.

europapark.de ↗

Theme park

LEGOLAND Billund

Billund, Denmark

The original LEGOLAND, opened in 1968, a short walk from LEGO House, the brick-lover’s experience centre.

Ages 2–12The original LEGOLANDLEGO House nearby
  • Combine LEGOLAND and LEGO House in one trip to make the travel worthwhile
  • Check seasonal opening: the park isn’t open all year

Scout says: A dream trip for LEGO fans. Book LEGO House separately: it’s its own attraction with its own tickets.

legoland.dk ↗

Theme park resort

PortAventura World

Salou, Costa Dorada, Spain

A beach-holiday-friendly resort with PortAventura Park, Ferrari Land and Caribe Aquatic Park, near the Costa Dorada beaches.

Ages 4+Beach holidayWater park
  • Hotel stays often include park access: compare packages with separate tickets
  • Combine with a beach holiday rather than a standalone trip

Scout says: Perfect for a sun-and-rides holiday. Pack the suncream and plan indoor breaks in the hottest hours.

Theme park

Parc Astérix

Plailly, near Paris, France

A big, funny, very French park themed on the Astérix comics, with strong coasters and family rides.

Ages 4+Near ParisCoasters
  • Online, date-based tickets are usually cheaper than the gate
  • Easy to combine with a Paris city break

Scout says: A great second park if you’re already doing Disneyland Paris, and a different flavour of fun.

Historic amusement park

Tivoli Gardens

Copenhagen, Denmark

One of the world’s oldest amusement parks (opened 1843), in the heart of Copenhagen, with gardens, rides and festive seasonal lights.

All agesCity centreSeasonal events
  • Entry and rides are priced separately: an unlimited-ride wristband suits ride-hungry kids, single entry suits strollers
  • Combine with a city break

Scout says: Visit after dark: the lights are the real star. Halloween and Christmas seasons are magical.

All agesPandasLoire Valley

Theme parks

Walt Disney World Resort

Orlando, Florida, USA

Four theme parks (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom) plus two water parks. It’s a holiday, not a day out, so plan several days.

All agesMulti-dayUnder-3s free
  • Children under 3 don’t need a park ticket
  • Multi-day tickets cost less per day than single days
  • Check Disney’s official special offers page before booking

Scout says: Don’t try to do it all. Two parks done well beat four done in a rush, especially with little ones.

Disney deals guidedisneyworld.disney.go.com ↗

Theme parks

Universal Orlando Resort

Orlando, Florida, USA

Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe (opened 2025), plus Volcano Bay water park. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter is spread across all three theme parks.

Ages 5+Harry PotterEpic Universe
  • Multi-day tickets are better value per day
  • For 2026, 3-day-plus tickets include Epic Universe visits; 2-day tickets don’t, so check before you buy
  • You need a park-to-park ticket to ride the Hogwarts Express between the two original parks

Scout says: Harry Potter fans: Diagon Alley and Hogsmeade are in different parks. Get a park-to-park ticket or you’ll miss half the magic.

Harry Potter guideuniversalorlando.com ↗

Theme parks

Disneyland Resort

Anaheim, California, USA

The original Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure sit side by side, so it’s easier to tackle in two or three days than Florida.

All agesTwo parksWalkable
  • Under-3s go free
  • Multi-day tickets lower the cost per day
  • Southern California residents sometimes get special ticket offers: check Disney’s offers page

Scout says: The two parks are a short walk apart, so a park hopper ticket is easy to use here.

  • Entry rules: UK travellers visiting the EU now go through the Entry/Exit System, and ETIAS is due to follow. Visitors to the UK need an ETA. See EES & ETIAS explained and the UK ETA guide on TravelDeals.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best theme park in Europe for families?
Disneyland Paris, Efteling in the Netherlands and Europa-Park in Germany are all excellent for families. Efteling’s Fairytale Forest is especially good for young children.
Is it worth going to Orlando for theme parks?
Orlando has more major theme parks than anywhere else, so it suits families who want a whole holiday of parks. Plan several days and look for multi-day tickets.

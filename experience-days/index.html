How experience vouchers work

  1. You buy a voucher for a specific experience, or a “choice” voucher with several options.
  2. The recipient books a date, either with the voucher company or directly with the venue.
  3. Vouchers have an expiry date. Some can be extended or exchanged for a fee, so check the terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good experience day for a family?
Animal encounters, treetop adventures, behind-the-scenes tours and theatre trips work for most ages. Balloon flights and driving experiences suit older children and teens.
Do experience day vouchers expire?
Most do. Check the validity period before buying, and whether you can extend or exchange the voucher if plans change.

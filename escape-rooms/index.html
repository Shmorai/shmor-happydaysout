How escape rooms work

Your team is locked in a themed room and has to find clues and solve puzzles to get out, usually within 60 minutes. A game host watches and gives hints if you get stuck. Most rooms have a minimum age, and younger players often need an adult in the team.

Picking a room

  • Choose a beginner or family room for your first go.
  • Check if the theme is scary. Horror rooms often have higher age limits.
  • Look at group sizes: most rooms take 2 to 6 players.
  • Mobile escape games and puzzle boxes are a cheaper option at home.

Frequently asked questions

What age can kids do escape rooms?
It varies by venue and room. Many family rooms welcome children from around 8 to 10 with an adult, while scarier rooms have higher limits. Check before booking.
Are escape rooms good for teenagers?
Yes. They’re one of the most popular teen activities and work brilliantly for birthdays.

Scout’s theme park rule of thumb

Theme park

Alton Towers Resort

Staffordshire, England

The UK’s best-known thrill park, with big roller coasters for teens and CBeebies Land for little ones, plus a water park and hotels on site.

All agesThrill ridesCBeebies Land
  • Included in Merlin Annual Passes (check exclusion dates)
  • A Tesco Clubcard Reward Partner in 2026: check the Clubcard app for current exchanges

Scout says: Split the day: thrill rides first while queues are short, CBeebies Land after lunch when little legs need a slower pace.

LEGOLAND Windsor Resort

Windsor, Berkshire, England

Built for families with children aged roughly 2 to 12: gentle coasters, driving schools, MINILAND models and plenty of hands-on play.

Ages 2–12Gentle ridesHotels on site

Theme parks

Walt Disney World Resort

Orlando, Florida, USA

Four theme parks (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom) plus two water parks. It’s a holiday, not a day out, so plan several days.

All agesMulti-dayUnder-3s free
  • Children under 3 don’t need a park ticket
  • Multi-day tickets cost less per day than single days
  • Check Disney’s official special offers page before booking

Scout says: Don’t try to do it all. Two parks done well beat four done in a rush, especially with little ones.

Orlando, Florida, USA

How to save on theme park tickets

  • Book online, in advance. Most parks use date-based pricing, and buying on the gate is usually the most expensive way in.
  • Go off-peak. Term-time weekdays are often cheaper and quieter than weekends and school holidays.
  • Check who goes free. Under-3s are free at most big parks, including Disney and LEGOLAND.
  • Use the train. The National Rail Days Out Guide lists rail-ticket discounts at some parks.
  • Think annual pass. If you’ll visit two or more Merlin parks in a year, compare the Merlin Annual Pass.
  • Use rewards. Tesco Clubcard vouchers and Kids Pass can cut the cost at some parks.

Making the most of the day

Arrive before opening, head to the most popular ride first, and save shows and gentle rides for the busy middle of the day. Ask about rider switch (also called baby swap) so grown-ups can take turns without queuing twice. Our theme park queue tips go into more detail.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best UK theme park for young children?
LEGOLAND Windsor and Paultons Park (home of Peppa Pig World) are designed with young children in mind. Chessington mixes gentle rides with a zoo, which suits mixed-age families.
Are under-3s free at theme parks?
At most big parks, yes, including Walt Disney World, Disneyland Paris and LEGOLAND. Always check the park’s own ticket page, because age rules vary.
Is it cheaper to buy theme park tickets on the day?
Usually not. Most parks charge the highest prices at the gate and reward booking online in advance, especially for quieter dates.

Water park tips

  • Check height limits on the biggest slides before you promise anything.
  • Bring flip-flops, a waterproof phone pouch and a padlock for lockers.
  • Younger children should wear arm bands or a swim vest if they can’t swim confidently, and always need supervision.

What is the best indoor water park in the UK?
Popular choices include Sandcastle Waterpark in Blackpool and the indoor pools at Center Parcs villages. Many local leisure centres also have flumes and wave machines.

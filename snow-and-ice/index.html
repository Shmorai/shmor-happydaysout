First-timer tips

  • Gloves are usually required on snow slopes and rinks.
  • Wear thick socks and layers. Helmets are often provided or recommended.
  • Skate aids help small children balance on the ice.

Can you ski indoors in the UK?
Yes. Indoor real-snow centres such as the Snowdome in Tamworth and Chill Factore in Manchester offer lessons, slope time and snow play.

Disney deals and offers for families

A Disney trip is a big-ticket day out. Here’s how families in the UK, US and Europe can save on Disneyland Paris, Walt Disney World and the original Disneyland, using official offers only.

Theme parks

Walt Disney World Resort

Orlando, Florida, USA

Four theme parks (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom) plus two water parks. It’s a holiday, not a day out, so plan several days.

All agesMulti-dayUnder-3s free
  • Children under 3 don’t need a park ticket
  • Multi-day tickets cost less per day than single days
  • Check Disney’s official special offers page before booking

Scout says: Don’t try to do it all. Two parks done well beat four done in a rush, especially with little ones.

Ways to save on Disney

  • Under-3s go free at Disneyland Paris, Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort.
  • Buy more days. At the US parks the price per day drops the more days you buy.
  • Avoid peak dates. School holidays, Christmas and Halloween weekends cost the most.
  • Compare packages. Hotel-and-ticket packages sometimes include extras like free dining for children. Compare them with booking separately.
  • Buy from Disney or an authorised seller. Unofficial tickets can be invalid.

Frequently asked questions

Are there Disney discount codes?
Disney doesn’t generally publish public discount codes. Genuine offers are listed on Disney’s official websites and apply when you book eligible dates and packages. Be wary of websites promising secret codes.
Do children under 3 need a Disney ticket?
No. Children under 3 go free at Disneyland Paris, Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort.
How many days do you need at Walt Disney World?
With four theme parks, most families plan at least four park days, and the price per day falls as you add days. Disneyland Paris and Disneyland Resort each have two parks, so two to three days works well.

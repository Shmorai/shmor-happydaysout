Staying healthy at farms

Farm animals can carry germs, so wash hands thoroughly with soap and water after touching animals and before eating, and supervise young children closely. Read pumpkin picking and toddler days out.

Frequently asked questions

When is lambing season?
Many UK farm parks have lambs in spring, typically from around February to April. Check with the farm, as it varies.

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London: tips for families

Walking into the Great Hall is a genuinely magical moment for Harry Potter fans of any age. Here’s how to plan a family visit to the Studio Tour, and how to keep the cost under control.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 9 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Aditya Vyas on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s Studio Tour tips

Book direct, book early and allow longer than you think. Most families spend 3–4 hours, and the big model of Hogwarts at the end is worth slowing down for.

  • Children aged 4 and under are free, but need a ticket
  • There are no tickets on arrival
  • Pack snacks: food inside adds up

On this page

  1. Booking tickets
  2. Getting there

Booking tickets

  • Book online in advance on the official website. Tickets are for a timed entry slot and can’t be bought on arrival.
  • Under-5s go free (aged 4 and under) but still need a ticket.
  • Book early for holidays: school holiday dates can sell out weeks or months ahead.
  • Watch out for resellers: packages with transport or hotels cost more. Compare with booking direct.

Getting there

The Studio Tour is in Leavesden, near Watford. By train, go to Watford Junction and take the shuttle bus. Check the official site for the current shuttle details. Driving? There’s on-site parking.

On the day

  • Arrive a little before your slot to allow for security.
  • Highlights include the Great Hall, the Forbidden Forest, Platform 9¾ and the Hogwarts Express, Diagon Alley and the giant model of Hogwarts.
  • There’s a café halfway round where you can try Butterbeer.
  • The gift shop is at the end, so agree a souvenir budget in advance!

Make a day of it

Combine the tour with a free photo at Platform 9¾ at King’s Cross and London’s free museums. Visiting from the US or Europe? Check the UK ETA rules before you fly.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Harry Potter Studio Tour take?
Most families spend around 3 to 4 hours. There’s no time limit once you’re in, so big fans can take longer.
Is there a discount for the Studio Tour?
Public discount codes are rare. Save by booking direct, choosing a quieter date and remembering that children aged 4 and under go free with a ticket.
Can you take a buggy on the Studio Tour?
The tour is step-free and buggies are generally allowed, but check the official accessibility information before you visit.

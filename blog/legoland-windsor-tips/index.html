LEGOLAND Windsor tips: plan your day and save

LEGOLAND Windsor is built for families with younger children, and it’s one of the UK’s most popular days out. Here’s how to plan a brilliant, better-value visit.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 1 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Sen on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s LEGOLAND route

Most families stop at the first rides they see. Head to the far side of the park first and work back towards the entrance as the day goes on.

  • Measure your kids before you go: height limits decide everything
  • Bring a change of clothes for the water rides
  • Under-3s are typically free

Who it suits

LEGOLAND is designed for families with children aged roughly 2 to 12. Many rides are gentle, and lots let younger children ride with an adult. Older teens may prefer thrill parks.

Ways to save

  • Book online, ahead: date-based pricing rewards quieter days and early booking.
  • Merlin Annual Pass: LEGOLAND Windsor is included. See if a pass pays off.
  • Rail offers: in 2026 the Days Out Guide listed a rail-ticket discount here. Check the live listing.
  • Bring a picnic: food inside theme parks adds up quickly.

Plan your day

  • Arrive before opening and head deeper into the park first.
  • See MINILAND in the busy middle of the day. It doesn’t need queuing.
  • Use the app for live queue times.
  • Ask about the Ride Access Pass in advance if anyone in your group needs it. See our queue tips.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

What age is LEGOLAND Windsor best for?
It’s designed for families with children aged roughly 2 to 12. Many rides suit younger children riding with an adult.
Is LEGOLAND Windsor included in the Merlin Annual Pass?
Yes. LEGOLAND Windsor Resort is included in Merlin Annual Passes, subject to each pass’s exclusion dates and pre-booking.

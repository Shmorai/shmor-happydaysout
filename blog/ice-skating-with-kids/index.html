Ice skating with kids: first-timer tips

Wobbly legs, big grins and a hot chocolate afterwards. Ice skating is a winter classic, and many rinks are open all year round.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 29 August 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Natalya Karpeka on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s skating tip

Teach kids to bend their knees and fall sideways, not backwards. Skate aids (those penguin or seal helpers) are a game-changer for little ones.

  • Gloves are a must
  • Off-peak sessions are calmer
  • Book festive rinks early

Year-round and festive rinks

Leisure centres and arenas often have public sessions all year. In winter, pop-up outdoor rinks appear at castles, museums and city squares, and at events like Hyde Park Winter Wonderland.

What to wear

  • Gloves (many rinks require them).
  • Warm, flexible layers and thick socks.
  • A helmet is a smart idea for young children.

Saving money

Public sessions at regular rinks are usually cheaper than festive pop-ups, and weekday daytime sessions cost less. More in snow and ice.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

What age can kids ice skate?
Many rinks welcome young children, often with skate aids. Check the rink’s minimum age and smallest skate sizes.

An advent calendar of days out

Swap chocolate for memories. Here are 24 festive ideas for December, one for each door. Lots are free, and some are quick enough for a school night.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 15 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Cole Ciarlello on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s advent plan

Mix tiny treats (a light walk, hot chocolate) with two or three big ones you book early. Not every door needs a ticket!

Doors 1–8: free and easy

  1. Walk to see the town Christmas lights
  2. Library festive story time
  3. Carol concert at a local church
  4. Christmas lights “safari” by car or on foot
  5. Frosty woodland walk with flask
  6. Visit a free museum’s winter trail
  7. Window-shopping for festive displays
  8. Make bird feeders and visit a nature reserve

Doors 9–16: small treats

  1. Ice skating session
  2. Christmas market wander. See markets with kids
  3. Kids’ cinema morning
  4. Bowling and hot chocolate
  5. Pick a real Christmas tree at a farm
  6. Gingerbread workshop
  7. Festive farm park day
  8. Light trail (book early)

Doors 17–24: the big ones

  1. Santa Special steam train
  2. Pantomime
  3. Santa’s grotto
  4. Castle or stately home Christmas event
  5. Winter wonderland
  6. Theme park Christmas event
  7. Festive escape room (teens!)
  8. Christmas Eve walk and doorstep carols

Plan the big ones with our Christmas days out guide and the adventure finder.

Thrill rides for teens: the best coaster parks

Got a coaster-obsessed teenager? These are the parks with the biggest drops, loops and launches, plus tips to keep younger siblings and nervous parents happy too.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 13 September 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Hannes Knutsson on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s thrill tip

Teens can often ride in their own group while parents do gentler rides with siblings. Agree a meeting point and time before you split up.

  • Check height limits on the park website
  • Use single rider queues
  • Ride the biggest coaster first thing

UK thrill parks

  • Thorpe Park (Surrey): built around big thrill rides.
  • Alton Towers (Staffordshire): famous coasters plus areas for little ones.
  • Blackpool Pleasure Beach (Lancashire): historic wooden coasters and big modern rides.

Europe and the US

  • Phantasialand (Germany) and Europa-Park (Germany) for richly themed coasters.
  • PortAventura (Spain), including Ferrari Land.
  • Cedar Point (Ohio), a legendary coaster park, and Universal Orlando for themed thrills.

Read Europe’s best theme parks.

Height rules and safety

Every ride has a minimum height (and some a maximum). They’re strict, so measure before you go and check the park’s ride guide. People with certain medical conditions are often advised not to ride intense coasters: read the signs at each ride.

Queue hacks

Arrive before opening, use single rider lines and save the big ride for the last hour too. More in theme park queue tips.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best UK theme park for teenagers?
Thorpe Park and Alton Towers are the UK’s best-known thrill parks, with Blackpool Pleasure Beach another favourite.
Can teenagers go round a theme park alone?
Rules vary. Many parks require under-16s or under-14s to be accompanied. Check the park’s age policy.

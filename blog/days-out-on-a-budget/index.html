Family days out on a budget: 15 ways to spend less

A brilliant day out doesn’t need a big budget. These are Scout’s favourite money-saving habits, all simple and all real.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 20 September 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Liviu Boldis on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s top three

If you only do three things: book online ahead, pack a picnic and check who goes free. Those alone can save a family a lot on a big day out.

Before you go

  1. Book online: gate prices are usually the highest.
  2. Go off-peak: term-time weekdays cost less at date-priced attractions.
  3. Check who goes free: under-3s and under-5s often do. See kids go free.
  4. Use the train for 2FOR1: see how it works.
  5. Get a railcard for family train travel.
  6. Compare passes if you’ll go back: annual pass vs day tickets.
  7. Use rewards: supermarket points and discount apps.

On the day

  1. Pack a picnic and refillable bottles: checklist here.
  2. Set a souvenir budget before you arrive.
  3. Skip the photo packages unless they’re really wanted.
  4. Park smart: compare parking with park-and-ride or public transport.
  5. Bring your own buggy and rain gear instead of hiring or buying.

Choose free fun

  1. Free museums: see free days out.
  2. Beaches, forests and parks with a picnic.
  3. Free events: heritage open days, library activities and local festivals.

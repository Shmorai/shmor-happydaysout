Christmas light trails with kids: how to pick one and save money

Light trails are one of the easiest winter days out to love, and one of the easiest to overpay for. Here is how to choose a trail that suits your children, book smartly and avoid the common disappointments.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 24 November 2026 · 3 min read

Photo: Oast House Archive / CC BY-SA 2.0

Scout’s tip

Scout’s light-trail tip

Pick the quietest slot you can. An early or midweek entry time means shorter queues, easier pushchair access and children who are not yet overtired.

  • Book ahead: popular time slots can sell out
  • Look for off-peak ticket dates before you pay peak prices
  • Check the age and child rules on the venue’s own page

What a Christmas light trail really involves

Most trails are an evening walk of a mile or so through a garden, park or estate, with lit-up installations, music and often food stalls. That means you are outdoors in December, after dark, usually for an hour or two. They work best for children old enough to walk most of the route, or for babies and toddlers in a pushchair on a surfaced path.

Treat it as an event rather than a visit: tickets are normally for a fixed time slot, and the price often reflects the evening, not the garden.

RHS Glow 2026: dates and how booking works

As of November 2026, the Royal Horticultural Society lists Glow at all five of its gardens, on selected dates only:

  • RHS Wisley, Surrey: 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, including Christmas Eve.
  • RHS Bridgewater, Salford: 26 November to 30 December 2026.
  • RHS Harlow Carr, North Yorkshire: 26 November to 30 December 2026.
  • RHS Hyde Hall, Essex: 26 November to 30 December 2026.
  • RHS Rosemoor, North Devon: 27 November to 30 December 2026.

The RHS says to book ahead for the best availability and prices, and that popular time slots sell out. It also offers Off Peak and Super Off Peak tickets on a limited number of dates, and RHS members pay reduced Glow prices. Child and under-5 rules are not on the main page, so read the venue’s own Glow page and terms before you buy. Dates and prices can change, so check the official RHS Glow page.

How to save money on light trails

  • Choose off-peak dates. Where a venue offers cheaper weekday or later-season tickets, they are usually the biggest saving.
  • Check who pays what. Children’s prices, free under-5s and family tickets vary by venue, so compare the total for your family, not the headline adult price.
  • Count the extras. Parking, hot chocolate and glow toys add up. Eat before you go and agree a treat budget with the children.
  • Look at free alternatives. Many towns run free switch-on events and illuminated streets, which pair well with our Christmas markets with kids guide.

More ways to cut the cost of winter outings are in days out on a budget and our round-up of kids-go-free offers.

Keeping children happy on a cold night

This is our practical advice rather than venue rules:

  • Wear layers, hats and gloves, and bring spare dry socks for the car.
  • Pack a small torch or glow bracelet so children can find you in a crowd, if the venue allows them.
  • Take a flask. Hot drinks at event prices are the quickest way to blow the budget.
  • Walk the route in the direction of the crowd and stop at the quieter installations first.
  • Agree a meeting point on arrival in case you get separated.

Building a light trail into a bigger festive plan

A trail makes a good finale to a December weekend. Pair it with a daytime activity from our Christmas days out for families guide, or slot it into the advent calendar of days out. If you are travelling a long way, book the trail first and build the rest of the day around your time slot.

Frequently asked questions

When does RHS Glow 2026 start?
As of November 2026, RHS Wisley’s Glow runs from 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 on selected dates. Bridgewater, Harlow Carr and Hyde Hall run from 26 November to 30 December, and Rosemoor from 27 November to 30 December. Check the RHS site for exact dates.
Do I need to book a Christmas light trail in advance?
For popular trails, yes. The RHS advises booking ahead and says popular time slots sell out. Off-peak slots are often easier to get and may be cheaper.
Are Christmas light trails suitable for toddlers and babies?
Often, if the path is suitable for a pushchair and you choose an early slot, but rules differ by venue. Check the venue’s own page for age, child-price and buggy details before you book.
Is there a cheaper way to see Christmas lights?
Yes. Many towns and cities have free switch-on events and illuminated streets, and ticketed trails often have cheaper off-peak dates. Compare the full cost for your family, including parking and food.

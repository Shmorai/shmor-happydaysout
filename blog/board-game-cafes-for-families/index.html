Board game cafés: a rainy day out for families and teens

Hundreds of games, someone to teach you the rules, and snacks on tap. Board game cafés are a brilliant rainy-day out that gets teens off their phones.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 15 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Zachary Keimig on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s game-night tip

Ask the staff for a recommendation. Tell them your ages and how long you’ve got, and they’ll pick a winner.

  • Book a table at weekends
  • Check the age policy (some are 18+ in the evening)
  • Quick party games suit mixed ages

How they work

Most board game cafés charge a per-person table or cover fee for a set time, with food and drinks on top. You can play anything from the library, and staff often teach the rules.

Games for every age

  • Little ones: simple co-operative games and matching games.
  • Ages 8+: quick card games and family strategy games.
  • Teens: party and bluffing games, plus bigger strategy games.

Make a day of it

Pair it with a museum or the cinema. See indoor fun, rainy day ideas and escape rooms for more puzzle fun.

Frequently asked questions

Are board game cafés good for kids?
Many welcome families during the day and have games for young children. Some are adults-only in the evenings, so check the age policy.

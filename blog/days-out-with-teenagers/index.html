Days out with teenagers they’ll actually enjoy

Teens can be the toughest crowd. The trick is giving them some choice and a bit of adrenaline. Here are days out that teenagers genuinely rate.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 15 September 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Hannes Knutsson on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s teen tip

Let them pick the day out, and maybe bring a friend. A teen who chose the plan is a teen who enjoys the plan.

  • Check when “child” tickets stop: often at 12 or 15
  • Students may get discounts with ID
  • Thrill parks: check height limits for younger siblings

On this page

  2. Something different

Thrill seekers

Big coasters at Thorpe Park and Alton Towers, or Europa-Park and PortAventura abroad. Arrive at opening and use single rider lines. See our queue tips.

Something different

  • Escape rooms and immersive games.
  • Indoor climbing, trampoline parks and go-karting.
  • Experience days like junior driving or indoor skydiving.
  • Studio tours, like the Harry Potter Studio Tour.

City days and outdoors

A day exploring a city (shopping, street food and a show) or a proper outdoor adventure (coasteering, kayaking or a mountain hike with a guide) often goes down better than “a nice walk”.

Saving on teen tickets

Many attractions charge adult prices from 12, 15 or 16, so check the age bands. Family tickets, rail 2FOR1 and annual passes help.

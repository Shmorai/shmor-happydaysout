Free museums in London for kids

London has some of the best museums on the planet, and many of them are free. Here are the family favourites and how to make the most of a free day out in the capital.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 4 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Claudio Testa on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s London plan

South Kensington is museum heaven: the Natural History Museum, Science Museum and V&A sit side by side. Pick one or two, not all three, with little ones.

  • Book free timed tickets at busy times
  • Arrive at opening for the dinosaurs
  • Special exhibitions and some activities charge

Natural History Museum

Dinosaurs, a blue whale skeleton in Hintze Hall, and an earthquake simulator. General admission is free. nhm.ac.uk

Science Museum

Space, flight and hands-on galleries. General admission is free. Some interactive galleries and special exhibitions charge. sciencemuseum.org.uk

Young V&A

In Bethnal Green, east London, the Young V&A is a free museum designed for children and young people, packed with play and design. vam.ac.uk/young

British Museum

Egyptian mummies, the Rosetta Stone and treasures from around the world. General admission is free. britishmuseum.org

Make it a full day

Travel in by train and use the 2FOR1 offers for a paid attraction, grab a free photo at Platform 9¾ at King’s Cross, and picnic in a royal park. Visiting from abroad? See London city breaks.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

Is the Natural History Museum free?
Yes. General admission is free. Some special exhibitions charge, and you may want to book a free timed ticket at busy times.
What is the best free museum in London for toddlers?
The Young V&A in Bethnal Green is designed for children, and the Natural History Museum’s dinosaurs are a hit with all ages.

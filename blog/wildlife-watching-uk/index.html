Wildlife watching in the UK

Puffins with rainbow beaks, seals lounging on rocks and deer in the autumn mist. Britain’s wild animals make for a brilliant family day out.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 20 August 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Daiga Ellaby on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s wildlife code

Keep your distance, stay quiet and never feed wild animals. Binoculars make it more exciting than getting closer!

  • Puffins are seasonal: roughly spring to midsummer
  • Use licensed boat trip operators
  • Autumn is deer rutting season

What to see and when

  • Puffins: nest on islands and cliffs from around spring into midsummer.
  • Seals: visible on many coasts year-round. Some areas see pups in autumn and winter.
  • Dolphins: seen from some coasts and boat trips.
  • Deer: the autumn rut is dramatic. Watch from a safe distance.

Boat trips

Choose operators who follow wildlife watching codes, and check age rules and weather cancellations. Pack layers. It’s colder at sea.

Frequently asked questions

When can you see puffins in the UK?
Puffins come ashore to nest from around spring until midsummer, then head back out to sea. Check with local reserves for the best times.

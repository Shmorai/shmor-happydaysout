Teen birthday day out ideas

Too old for a bouncy castle, too young to go out alone? Here are birthday days out that teens will happily invite their mates to.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 12 September 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Alexander Andrews on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s party tip

Ask about group or party packages. They often include extras like food or private time, and can work out cheaper per person.

  • Book weekends early
  • Check the youngest guest meets age limits
  • Collect consent forms ahead if the venue needs them

Active and competitive

  • Go-karting: junior sessions with a podium finish.
  • Laser tag: team battles in neon arenas.
  • Trampoline parks: dodgeball courts and foam pits.
  • Climbing or bouldering: a taster session with instructors.

Brainy and social

  • Escape rooms: perfect for small groups.
  • Bowling plus pizza: a classic that still works.
  • A city day with a show or a studio tour.

Big-ticket treats

A thrill park, a surf lesson or indoor skydiving makes a birthday unforgettable. Gift experiences can also be shared: see experience days.

Frequently asked questions

What can I do for a 13th birthday?
Popular choices include escape rooms, go-karting, laser tag, trampoline parks, climbing walls and theme parks. Let them choose and invite a few friends.

The best days out with toddlers

Toddlers don’t need big rides. They need animals, water, things to push and space to run. Here are the best days out for little ones, and how to keep everyone smiling.

By the HappyDaysOut editorial team · 16 September 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Xavi Cabrera on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s toddler tactics

Plan around the nap, not the attraction. A shorter morning out with a buggy nap on the way home beats a long day that ends in tears.

  • Many attractions let under-2s or under-3s in free
  • Check buggy access and baby-change facilities
  • Pack spare clothes, always

Toddler-friendly days out

  • Farm parks and petting farms: animals, tractors and play barns. See local days out.
  • Aquariums: short, indoor and mesmerising. See zoos and aquariums.
  • Gentle theme parks: Peppa Pig World at Paultons Park and LEGOLAND.
  • Steam trains: see heritage railways.
  • Free museums with play areas, like the Young V&A.

Saving money with toddlers

Under-3s go free at most theme parks, and under-5s are free at many heritage sites. Check our kids go free guide. A toddler-only trip during term time is often the cheapest and quietest of all.

Survival kit

  • Snacks, then more snacks.
  • Spare clothes and nappies or pull-ups.
  • A favourite toy or comforter.
  • Sun hat or waterproofs, depending on the sky.
  • A lightweight buggy or carrier.

Frequently asked questions

What are good days out for 2-year-olds?
Farm parks, aquariums, gentle theme parks like Peppa Pig World, steam train rides and playgrounds. Short trips that fit around naps work best.

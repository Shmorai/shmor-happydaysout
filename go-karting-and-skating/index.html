Safety first

Wear a helmet for skating, scooting and cycling, and pads for beginners. At karting tracks, follow the briefing and the marshals. Read go-karting for kids, skate parks and scooter days and family cycling days out.

What age can children go karting?
It depends on the track. Many offer junior sessions with smaller karts, with minimum ages and heights. Check the track’s rules before booking.

Days out near you: local family day trip ideas

The best days out are often just down the road. Here’s how to find great local family days out wherever you live, and the kinds of places that work for every age.

Photo: Duong Thinh on Unsplash

Scout’s tip

Scout’s local-day-out trick

Search your council’s website and your local tourist board for “events this weekend”. They list free fun days, farmers’ markets and festivals that big sites miss.

  • Check opening days: small attractions often close midweek in winter
  • Look for under-3s free or family tickets
  • Pack a picnic and save on lunch

How to find days out near you

  • Tourist boards: VisitEngland, VisitScotland, Visit Wales and Discover Northern Ireland list attractions by area. In the US, state tourism sites and state parks do the same.
  • Membership maps: the National Trust and English Heritage have maps of properties near you.
  • Rail offers: the Days Out Guide lets you search attractions near a station.
  • Search HappyDaysOut: try the search box with a type of day out, like “zoo” or “steam train”.

Bringing the dog?

Many country parks, beaches (outside seasonal restrictions) and National Trust gardens welcome dogs on leads. If the dog can’t come, our sister site PetSavings covers dog walkers and pet sitters.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find family days out near me?
Check your local tourist board, council events listings, National Trust and English Heritage maps, and the National Rail Days Out Guide. Local parenting groups are great for recommendations too.
What are good local days out for toddlers?
Farm parks, soft play, aquariums, local parks with playgrounds and story time at the library. Short trips with plenty of snack stops work best.

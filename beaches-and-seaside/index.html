Beach safety

In the UK, lifeguarded beaches use red and yellow flags to mark the safest area to swim. Never swim at a red flag. If someone is in trouble in the water, call 999 and ask for the Coastguard. Read beach safety for families and the rock pooling guide.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

What do the red and yellow flags mean on a beach?
They mark an area patrolled by lifeguards and the safest place to swim and bodyboard. Always swim between them.

Zoos & aquariums

Zoos, safari parks and aquariums for families

Penguins at feeding time, giraffes at eye level and sharks overhead. Zoos and aquariums are a day out every age enjoys. Here are the best, with Scout’s tips.

Photo: Daley van de Sande on Unsplash

Scout’s verdict

Scout’s zoo verdict

Arrive early, when the animals are most active, and plan your route around feeding times and keeper talks. Aquariums are the perfect rainy-day backup.

Best for
Every age, especially under-10s
Best saving
Membership if you’re local
Top tip
Check feeding & talk times

Zoo

Chester Zoo

Chester, England

One of the UK’s biggest zoos, run by a conservation charity, with a huge site to explore. Expect a full day.

All agesFull dayCharity zoo
  • Book online in advance
  • Membership can pay off if you live nearby and visit a few times a year

Scout says: Wear comfy shoes and bring snacks: it’s a big site with lots of walking.

London Zoo

Regent’s Park, London

The world’s oldest scientific zoo, run by the conservation charity ZSL, in Regent’s Park.

All agesCentral LondonCharity zoo
  • Listed on the National Rail Days Out Guide in 2026 (you need valid National Rail tickets)
  • Combine with a free picnic in Regent’s Park

Scout says: The Days Out Guide offer needs paper or e-tickets from National Rail; Oyster and contactless don’t count.

Safari park

Longleat

Wiltshire, England

The UK’s first drive-through safari park outside Africa, plus a stately home, gardens and adventure play.

All agesDrive-through safariFull day
  • Book online ahead
  • Its Festival of Light (in winter) is a separate seasonal event: check dates

Scout says: Keep windows up where the signs say so: the monkeys love a car aerial!

Edinburgh, Scotland

A hillside zoo run by the Royal Zoological Society of Scotland, famous for its penguins.

Aquariums

SEA LIFE aquariums

Across the UK, Europe and the US

Merlin’s chain of aquariums, with rainy-day-friendly indoor visits of a couple of hours. Great for toddlers.

Toddlers+IndoorRainy day

Ways to save at zoos

  • Book online: most zoos charge less online and use timed entry at busy times.
  • Memberships: a year’s membership can cost little more than two visits for local families.
  • Rail offers: some zoos appear on the Days Out Guide.
  • Gift Aid tickets: charity zoos often show a price with an optional donation first. The standard ticket is usually available too.
  • Reciprocal discounts: some zoo memberships give discounts at partner zoos. Check your membership’s benefits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to visit a zoo?
First thing in the morning, when animals are more active and crowds are smaller. Check feeding and keeper talk times as soon as you arrive.
Is a zoo membership worth it?
If you live nearby and expect to visit more than twice a year, a membership often works out cheaper, and supports the zoo’s conservation work.

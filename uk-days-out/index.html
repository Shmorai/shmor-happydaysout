UK days out

The best family days out in the UK

From LEGOLAND to Longleat and the Natural History Museum to Alnwick Castle, here are Britain’s best-loved family days out, with real ways to save on each.

Theme park

LEGOLAND Windsor Resort

Windsor, Berkshire, England

Built for families with children aged roughly 2 to 12: gentle coasters, driving schools, MINILAND models and plenty of hands-on play.

Ages 2–12Gentle ridesHotels on site
  • Included in Merlin Annual Passes
  • Listed on the National Rail Days Out Guide with a rail-ticket discount in 2026
  • Weekdays outside school holidays are usually the quietest and cheapest

Scout says: Under-3s go free, so it’s great value while they’re tiny. Check ride height limits before you promise anything!

Our LEGOLAND tipslegoland.co.uk ↗
UK

Photo: Hannes Knutsson / Unsplash

Theme park

Alton Towers Resort

Staffordshire, England

The UK’s best-known thrill park, with big roller coasters for teens and CBeebies Land for little ones, plus a water park and hotels on site.

All agesThrill ridesCBeebies Land
  • Included in Merlin Annual Passes (check exclusion dates)
  • A Tesco Clubcard Reward Partner in 2026: check the Clubcard app for current exchanges
  • Book online before you go: gate prices are usually the highest

Scout says: Split the day: thrill rides first while queues are short, CBeebies Land after lunch when little legs need a slower pace.

Merlin pass guidealtontowers.com ↗
UK

Photo: Ethan Hoover / Unsplash

Theme park + zoo

Chessington World of Adventures

Chessington, Surrey, England

A rare mix of family rides and a zoo in one ticket, which makes it a strong all-rounder for mixed-age families.

Ages 3+Zoo + ridesNear London
  • Included in Merlin Annual Passes
  • Animal areas are a calm break when queues build up

Scout says: Two days out for the price of one: plan your route around the animal talks and feeding times.

Merlin pass guidechessington.com ↗

Theme park

Paultons Park, home of Peppa Pig World

New Forest, Hampshire, England

Peppa Pig World is a toddler favourite, and the park’s new Viking-themed land, Valgard, opened in spring 2026 with bigger rides for older kids.

ToddlersPeppa Pig WorldValgard (2026)
  • Not part of the Merlin pass: check Paultons’ own annual passes if you live nearby
  • Closed in January and on some winter dates, so check the calendar first

Scout says: Peppa Pig World is busiest first thing. Head there in the afternoon and do the rest of the park in the morning.

Toddler days outpaultonspark.co.uk ↗
UK

Photo: Aditya Vyas / Unsplash

Studio tour

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London – The Making of Harry Potter

Leavesden, near Watford, England

Walk through the real sets, costumes and props from the films, including the Great Hall, Diagon Alley and the Hogwarts Express on Platform 9¾.

Ages 5+Pre-book onlyUnder-5s free
  • Children aged 4 and under go free, but still need a ticket
  • Book direct on the official site to avoid reseller mark-ups
  • Tickets can’t be bought on arrival, and school holidays sell out early

Scout says: Allow at least 3–4 hours. Save room for Butterbeer, and don’t rush the Gringotts and Hogwarts model sections at the end.

Studio Tour tipswbstudiotour.co.uk ↗

Free museum

Natural History Museum

South Kensington, London

Dinosaurs, a blue whale skeleton and an earthquake room. General admission is free.

All agesFree entryRainy day
  • General admission is free (some special exhibitions charge)
  • Book a free timed slot online at busy times

Scout says: The Science Museum and V&A are next door, so you can mix and match one free day.

Free London museumsnhm.ac.uk ↗

Zoo

Chester Zoo

Chester, England

One of the UK’s biggest zoos, run by a conservation charity, with a huge site to explore. Expect a full day.

All agesFull dayCharity zoo
  • Book online in advance
  • Membership can pay off if you live nearby and visit a few times a year

Scout says: Wear comfy shoes and bring snacks: it’s a big site with lots of walking.

Zoo

London Zoo

Regent’s Park, London

The world’s oldest scientific zoo, run by the conservation charity ZSL, in Regent’s Park.

All agesCentral LondonCharity zoo
  • Listed on the National Rail Days Out Guide in 2026 (you need valid National Rail tickets)
  • Combine with a free picnic in Regent’s Park

Scout says: The Days Out Guide offer needs paper or e-tickets from National Rail; Oyster and contactless don’t count.

Longleat

Wiltshire, England

The UK’s first drive-through safari park outside Africa, plus a stately home, gardens and adventure play.

All agesDrive-through safariFull day

  • Its Festival of Light (in winter) is a separate seasonal event: check dates

Castle & film location

Alnwick Castle

Northumberland, England

The castle used for Hogwarts exteriors in the first two Harry Potter films. Families can join broomstick training in the very courtyard from the movies.

Ages 4+Film locationBroomstick training
  • Check whether tickets can be upgraded to an annual pass for free on the official site
  • Pair with Alnwick Garden next door

Scout says: Broomstick training is pure joy for young witches and wizards. Check session times when you arrive.

Filming locationsalnwickcastle.com ↗

Heritage railway

The Jacobite steam train

Fort William to Mallaig, Scotland

The steam train that crosses the Glenfinnan Viaduct, famous from the Harry Potter films. It runs seasonally and sells out well ahead.

All agesSeasonalBook early
  • You can watch the train cross the viaduct for free from the Glenfinnan viewpoints
  • Book as early as possible for summer dates

Scout says: Can’t get tickets? Watching it cross the viaduct from the hillside is free and just as photogenic.

Steam train days outwestcoastrailways.co.uk ↗

Frequently asked questions

What is the most popular family day out in the UK?
Big favourites include the Natural History Museum and Science Museum (free), LEGOLAND Windsor, Alton Towers, Chester Zoo and the Warner Bros. Studio Tour London.
How can I save on UK attractions?
Travel by train and use the Days Out Guide 2FOR1 offers, consider an annual pass if you’ll visit often, look for under-5s-free rules and book online ahead of time.

Studio tour

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London – The Making of Harry Potter

Leavesden, near Watford, England

Walk through the real sets, costumes and props from the films, including the Great Hall, Diagon Alley and the Hogwarts Express on Platform 9¾.

Ages 5+Pre-book onlyUnder-5s free
  • Children aged 4 and under go free, but still need a ticket
  • Book direct on the official site to avoid reseller mark-ups
  • Tickets can’t be bought on arrival, and school holidays sell out early

Scout says: Allow at least 3–4 hours. Save room for Butterbeer, and don’t rush the Gringotts and Hogwarts model sections at the end.

The Jacobite steam train

Fort William to Mallaig, Scotland

The steam train that crosses the Glenfinnan Viaduct, famous from the Harry Potter films. It runs seasonally and sells out well ahead.

All agesSeasonalBook early
  • You can watch the train cross the viaduct for free from the Glenfinnan viewpoints
  • Book as early as possible for summer dates

Scout says: Can’t get tickets? Watching it cross the viaduct from the hillside is free and just as photogenic.

Universal Orlando Resort

Orlando, Florida, USA

Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe (opened 2025), plus Volcano Bay water park. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter is spread across all three theme parks.

Ages 5+Harry PotterEpic Universe
  • Multi-day tickets are better value per day
  • For 2026, 3-day-plus tickets include Epic Universe visits; 2-day tickets don’t, so check before you buy

Warner Bros. Studio Tour London: the essentials

  • Where: Leavesden, near Watford, about 20 miles north-west of central London. Shuttle buses run from Watford Junction station.
  • Tickets: timed entry, booked in advance only. Children aged 4 and under go free, but still need a ticket.
  • How long: most families spend 3 to 4 hours.
  • When: school holidays and weekends sell out early, so book as soon as you know your dates.

Read Scout’s full Studio Tour tips for families. Visiting from the US or Europe? You’ll need a UK ETA before you fly.

The Wizarding World of Harry Potter parks

Universal runs Wizarding World areas in Orlando, Hollywood and Japan. In Orlando, Hogsmeade is at Islands of Adventure, Diagon Alley is at Universal Studios Florida, and the newest land (the Ministry of Magic) is at Epic Universe. Frequently asked questions

Is there a discount code for the Harry Potter Studio Tour?
Genuine public discount codes are rare. The most reliable savings are booking direct on the official website (avoiding reseller mark-ups), choosing an off-peak date and remembering that children aged 4 and under go free with a ticket.
Can you buy Harry Potter Studio Tour tickets on the day?
No. Tickets must be booked in advance online for a timed entry slot. Popular dates, especially school holidays, can sell out weeks or months ahead.
Is the Studio Tour suitable for young children?
Children of any age can visit, and under-5s go free. It’s a walking tour of sets and props rather than rides, so it suits children who know the films, usually from about age 5 upwards.
